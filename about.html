About Credit Quest Effective date: 20 November 2025 Our mission Credit Quest helps young people build real-world money skills through short lessons and fun quizzes. We turn tricky topics like credit, borrowing, and bills into simple, interactive challenges. What we do • Learn: Bite-size lessons written in plain English for the UK. • Play: Quizzes that reinforce good habits (e.g., paying on time, using credit responsibly). • Progress: Earn in-app coins and trophies. Coins have no cash value. • No credit checks: We do not access your credit file and do not provide credit products.
