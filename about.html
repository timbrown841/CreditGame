About Credit Quest

Effective date: 20 November 2025

Credit Quest helps young people learn about credit with simple lessons and fun quizzes.

Our mission

  • Credit Quest helps young people build real-world money skills through short lessons and fun quizzes. We turn tricky topics like credit, borrowing, and bills into simple, interactive challenges.

What we do

  1. Learn: Bite-size lessons written in plain English for the UK.
  2. Play: Quizzes that reinforce good habits (e.g., paying on time, using credit responsibly).
  3. Progress: Earn in-app coins and trophies. Coins have no cash value.
  4. No credit checks: We do not access your credit file and do not provide credit products.
