🧠 Key topics

🏦
What banks do
Keep money safe, help pay/transfer, and lend to people/businesses.
💼
What a bank account is
A record of your money. You can pay in, take out, and track it in an app.
💳
Debit card vs cash
Debit card spends your own money from your account (not borrowing).
🏧
Using ATMs safely
Cover your PIN, check the slot, be aware of people around you.
