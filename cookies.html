Do we use cookies?

  • Right now, Credit Quest does not set non-essential cookies. The app relies primarily on localStorage to remember gameplay progress on your device.

Essential storage

  • localStorage (essential): Saves gameplay state (e.g., coins, levels, trophies) so the app works smoothly. Clearing your browser storage will reset your in-app progress on that device.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: We load Google Fonts (Fredoka). Font delivery generally does not set cookies for end users but requires basic technical data (e.g., IP address) to serve the font.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (frontend) and Render (backend) may place strictly necessary cookies for service delivery/security. We do not control these platforms’ cookies.

Children & consent

  • We do not set analytics/marketing cookies for child users without consent.
  • If we later introduce analytics, we will show a clear banner, obtain consent first, and let you change your preferences at any time.

Managing cookies

  • You can control cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect how the app works.
