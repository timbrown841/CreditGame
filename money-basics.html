🏦 Introduction to youth bank accounts

💳
Debit card (not credit)
Spend from your own money only. No borrowing.
🔐
Parent/guardian approval
Adults help open and keep an eye on things.
📱
App & notifications
Track spending, set limits, and get alerts.
🧠
Safety first
Keep your PIN private; ask an adult if unsure.

💬 Example interactions

Hi! I’m your bank helper. Want to set a savings goal?
Yes! I want to save for trainers.
Great choice. We’ll set a target and track your progress.

Try asking about PIN safety, notifications, or spending limits.
