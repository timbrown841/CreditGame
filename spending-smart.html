🛒 Spending Smart

• Financial Decisions

Make clever spending choices: compare deals, spot tricks, and plan before you buy.

🧠 Key ideas

🔍
Compare prices
Work out the unit price (e.g. p per 100g) to find the best value.
🏷️
Discounts ≠ always cheaper
“Was £10” might be a trick. Check today’s unit price.
📝
Plan your spend
Impulse buys eat your budget. Make a simple list first.
🤝
Need vs want
Ask: Do I need it now? Will I still want it next week?
