💡 Tips to Improve Your Credit Score

1. Always Pay On Time

Pay your bills, like your phone or Netflix, on time every month. This shows you're responsible with money!

2. Keep Balances Low

Don't use too much of your available credit. Try to use less than 30% of your limit if you can.

3. Check Your Score Often

Use free tools like Credit Karma to check your score. It's important to catch mistakes early!

4. Don’t Apply Too Much

Applying for too many cards or loans can lower your score. Be selective and responsible.

5. Build a Long History

The longer you use credit properly, the better. Start early and be consistent.
